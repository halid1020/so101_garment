# What each input contributes — paragraph flow

- P1 opens and forward-links the contribution figures and the video.
- P2 the method, in behaviour: each input stream is suppressed in turn and the
  change in the planned action is measured, so a share is the size of the
  difference that stream makes rather than anything the model reports about
  itself. Say why a stochastic policy must have its sampler pinned, with the
  measured size of the unpinned variation against the effect -- that single
  comparison justifies the whole precaution.
- P3 THE NORMALISATION CAVEAT, before any cross-policy sentence: shares are
  normalised within a policy, so a column sums to one and only the ORDER of
  streams may be carried across policies. State it as a rule for reading the
  figure, not as a footnote.
- P4 what the ordering shows per family: which stream dominates, where the
  tactile channels sit, and how little of the total they carry even at their
  peak.
- P5 that contribution moves within an episode, which a pooled number hides --
  tactile share rises around contact. This is what the framewise video shows
  and the still figures cannot.
- P6 where attribution cannot be applied at all, and why that is a property of
  the architecture rather than a gap in the work. -> threats.
