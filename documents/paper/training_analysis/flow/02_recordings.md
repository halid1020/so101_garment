# The recordings and the split — paragraph flow

- P1 opens the section and forward-links the two figures.
- P2 the corpus: one task, one session, episodes and frames and rate, five
  cameras of which four are tactile. Visual: one frame per episode as a contact
  sheet, so the reader sees the variety rather than reading a count.
- P3 how the split is made, and the fact that decides how it must be read: the
  trainer holds out the LAST episodes of each task, not a random sample. State
  the consequence immediately -- anything that drifted across the session is
  inside the held-out set -- and forward-link the threats section rather than
  burying it there.
- P4 how many frames are actually scored, which is smaller than the frame count
  because scoring samples the episodes. Give the sampled numbers for both
  halves so no reader mistakes the corpus size for the evidence size.
