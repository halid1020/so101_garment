# Purpose

Everything needed to build one dual-arm SO-101 garment-manipulation rig, with
the quantities the current rig actually uses. Where more than one supplier was
found for the same part, every option is listed as its own row; only the `.0`
row of each group is counted in the grand total, and the alternatives are
marked *alternative — not counted*.

Quantities follow the live rig configuration in `src/conf/sensor_map.yaml`: two
leader arms and two follower arms, one central camera, two wrist cameras and
four fingertip tactile cameras.

# Notes on the prices

**Date.** Every price was read on 1 September 2026 and none of these suppliers
holds a price steady; re-check before ordering.

**Why some cells say TBC.** Three suppliers refuse automated price reads.
RS Online returns *Access Denied*, RobotShop UK returns HTTP 403, and an
AliExpress product page serves a script shell with the price loaded afterwards,
so no figure appears in the page itself. The two AliExpress and RobotShop
figures that do appear above (£257.99 and £271.98) are the ones supplied with
the original shopping list, not readings taken from the sites. No price has been
substituted from a different retailer, and no figure in the table is an
estimate. For reference only, and *not* used anywhere in the table: The Pi Hut
lists the same Arducam B0196 module at £28.80.

**Currency.** The Inno-Maker store prices in US dollars. $18.69 has been
converted at 1 USD = 0.73904 GBP (European Central Bank reference rate,
1 September 2026), giving £13.81. Card fees and any UK import VAT or handling
charge are not included.

**VAT.** Amazon UK, Bambu Lab UK, RS Online and UnmannedTechShop show
UK-inclusive prices. The AliExpress and Inno-Maker figures are pre-import;
budget for VAT and a handling fee on both.

**Shipping.**

# Fit checks worth doing before ordering

1. **End-cap groove size.** The strut (11.0) and the angle bracket (12.0) are
   both 8 mm groove; the end cap (14.0) is listed as a **6 mm** groove part for
   the same 30 × 30 mm profile. Confirm the variant before ordering, or the caps
   will not seat.
2. **Plywood size.** Line 4.0 is fifteen 254 × 254 mm squares, not a single
   board. It will not cover a 90 × 150 cm table on its own; it suits the payload
   surface rather than the table top.
3. **USB bandwidth, not port count.** The rig runs five cameras across three
   host controllers because a hub adds no bandwidth — every camera here is
   USB 2.0 and the budget is per controller. One powered hub is listed for power
   and cable management; it does not remove the need for enough host
   controllers.
